1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They can help a website remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how its features are used.

Similar technologies may include localStorage, sessionStorage, and other browser-based mechanisms. Their behavior depends on how a website implements them.

ℹ️ CryptoKit uses the minimum amount of browser storage needed to deliver a secure and functional experience.

2 Types of Cookies

Essential Storage

Required to support core website functionality, security, and settings that you explicitly choose. These cannot be disabled.

Preference Storage

Remembers optional interface settings such as your selected theme (normal or hacker) or saved preferences.

Analytics & Optional Technologies

If analytics or optional third-party services are added, they will be disclosed and will only activate after you provide consent. No analytics scripts run before your consent where consent is required.

3 How We Use Cookies

Depending on the features enabled on the site, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:

  • Support website security and essential functionality
  • Remember your selected theme and interface preferences
  • Maintain session-related state where required
  • Understand performance and improve experience (only if analytics are enabled)
  • Persist your cookie consent choices across visits

🔐 CryptoKit does not use cookies to track you across unrelated websites.

5 Your Choices

You can manage cookies through:

  • Browser settings: Block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive notifications when cookies are set
  • Cookie preferences panel: Use the controls below (section 6) to save your optional preferences for this browser
  • Private / incognito mode: Browse without persistent storage between sessions

⚠️ Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the website from working correctly.

6 Cookie Preferences

Choose which optional categories you would like to allow. Essential storage is always on because the site cannot function without it. Your choices are stored locally in this browser.

7 Storage & Retention

  • Session cookies: Expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Remain until they expire or are deleted manually
  • Preference storage: Kept until you clear browser data or change your choice
  • Consent record: cryptokit_cookie_preferences_v1 stored in localStorage

🗂️ You can clear all CryptoKit storage at any time via your browser's site data settings.

8 Security

Where cookies are used, they follow modern security practices:

  • Secure flag for cookies set over HTTPS
  • SameSite attribute to mitigate cross-site request forgery
  • HttpOnly where applicable to protect from script access
  • Minimum data principle — only what is strictly necessary is stored

9 Policy Updates

We may update this policy when website features, technologies, or applicable requirements change. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date at the top.

ℹ️ Material changes may require you to review and re-save your preferences.
